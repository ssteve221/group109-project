## Decision making rules for disagreements

Default to majority vote among active team members. If a vote ties or a decision is time-critical, the Team Lead makes the final call within 2 hours to avoid blocking the board. All decisions affecting scope or Definition of Done are logged as a comment on the relevant board task.

## Conflict escalation path

If a disagreement or a stalled task isn't resolved through normal team discussion, it gets escalated so the sprint doesn't stall.

**Trigger — step 1 — step 2...**
```
Trigger: Disagreement unresolved after one team discussion, OR a member shows
zero visible board/commit activity for 2+ consecutive days.
Step 1: Direct 1:1 conversation between the involved members within 24 hours.
Step 2: If unresolved, raise it at the next stand-up for group mediation.
Step 3: If still unresolved within 48 hours, escalate to the instructor/mentor,
citing the audit log as evidence.
```
